# Glossary

- **Restaking:** reusing staked assets to secure additional services.
- **AVS:** Actively Validated Service.
- **Operator:** entity running software for AVSs with delegated stake.
- **LRT:** liquid restaking token.
- **Slashing:** penalty destroying part of a stake for misbehaviour.
- **Cost of corruption:** what an attacker loses by attacking.
- **Cascade:** a shock in one service propagating to others through shared stake.
- **Allocation:** the portion of an operator's stake made slashable by a specific service.
- **Correlation penalty:** an extra slashing penalty that grows with the number of validators slashed in the same period.
- **Effective security:** stake that is allocated, slashable and unable to exit before a slash executes.
- **Equivocation:** signing two conflicting messages for the same slot or task; the canonical objective slashing condition.
- **Looping:** repeatedly borrowing against a token to buy more of it, increasing leverage.
- **Profit from corruption:** the value an attacker can extract by corrupting one or more services in an attack window.
- **Slashing protection database:** a signer-side record that refuses to sign messages conflicting with earlier ones.
- **Veto committee:** a group that can cancel a slashing event, typically to protect against bugs.
- **Withdrawal delay:** the time between requesting and completing a withdrawal of restaked assets.
